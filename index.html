端午节是我国的传统节日。请写一篇英语短文介绍端午节：1. 时间和传统习俗；2. 屈原的爱国故事；3. 赛龙舟团队精神的现实意义。
2024年书面表达来源：2024年扬州中考真题
💡 点击查看答案与解析
✅ 参考范文
Duanwu Festival, also known as the Dragon Boat Festival, is one of the most important traditional festivals in China. It falls on the fifth day of the fifth lunar month every year. On this day, people usually eat zongzi and hold dragon boat races. Zongzi is a kind of sticky rice dumpling wrapped in bamboo leaves. Dragon boat racing is not only an exciting sport but also a symbol of teamwork. The festival is closely related to Qu Yuan, a great patriotic poet in ancient China. Qu Yuan sacrificed for his country, which showed us the spirit of patriotism. As a teenager, I believe it is our duty and responsibility to strive for the prosperity and strength of our motherland.
📖 解析
题材：材料作文 | 时态：一般现在时为主 | 结构：节日介绍→习俗→精神内涵
亮点词汇：traditional festival, is closely related to, strive for
高分句型：定语从句(which showed us...)、宾语从句(I believe it is...)

Q11秋天，树叶变黄，从树上落下。 _______________________________________________________________
2022年句子翻译来源：2022年扬州中考真题
💡 点击查看答案与解析
✅ 答案
In autumn, leaves turn yellow and fall off the trees.
📖 解析
【详解】 句子是陈述句，是一般现在时。表达“在秋天”用介词短语“ in autumn ”，是时间状语，放在句首。主语是“树叶”是复数概念，用复数名词“ leaves ”。表达“变”，用系动词“ turn ”。表达“黄”用形容词“ yellow ”作表语。表达“落下”用动词短语“ fall off ”，表达“树”用名词“ trees ”作宾语。用连词“ and ”连接动词形成并列结构。故填 In autumn, leaves turn yellow and fall off the trees.
